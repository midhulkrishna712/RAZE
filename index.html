The Beginning

Origin Story

🌑

Born in the Shadows

In the darkest corner of a war-torn realm, a boy was born during a lunar eclipse. Abandoned at birth, he was raised by outcasts and drifters who taught him one thing: the world is cruel, but the cruel don't have to win. He carried a blazing spirit—a symbol of the hope he refused to let go.

💥

The Forbidden Fruit

At fourteen, Raze accidentally consumed the Blaze-Blaze Fruit—a legendary artifact hidden by dark warlords. His body transformed, gaining the ability to stretch beyond human limits and ignite his fists with a spiritual fire fueled by his willpower. The darker forces tried to capture him. They failed.

🦸

The Guardian Rises

Now a relentless force of nature, Raze roams the world with a single promise: no one suffers alone. He hunts the shadow syndicates, dismantles corruption, and fights the monsters that normal people can't see. His glowing fist is now a beacon—when people see it, they know the darkness is about to be shattered.

Secret Identity

By day, Raze is just a wandering traveler who drifts from town to town, taking odd jobs and eating at every food stall he finds. People know him as the "Blazing Drifter"—a cheerful, harmless guy who can't seem to sit still. But when the shadows creep in and the darkness rises, his fiery aura becomes a war banner, and the grinning drifter transforms into the most feared guardian the dark world has ever known.
